## 🎯 Overview

**Word Association Test Online** is a modern, browser-based implementation of the classic **word association paradigm** — a psychological technique dating back to **Carl Jung** and **Francis Galton** that reveals how your mind links concepts, memories, and emotions.

You see a word, you respond with the first thing that comes to mind. Simple on the surface — but decades of research show these spontaneous links reveal patterns in memory, emotion, and cognition.

This platform brings the classic test into the modern age: instant scoring, semantic analysis, and exportable results for research or personal insight.

## 🧪 Test Types

### 🎯 Free Association

You see a word, respond instantly with whatever comes to mind.

**Best for:** Self-discovery, creative insight.

### ⏱️ Timed Association

Same as free but with a **strict time limit** (usually 3 seconds).

**Best for:** Research, reaction studies.

### 📋 Kent-Rosanoff

100 standardized words with **normative responses**.

**Best for:** Clinical comparison, research.

## 📊 Interpreting Results

### 🎯 Key Metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ⏱️ **Avg Reaction Time** | Processing speed |
| 🎨 **Semantic Distance** | Uniqueness vs norms |
| 🔁 **Repetitions** | Perseveration |
| 🚫 **Blocks** | Emotional interference |
| 📊 **Valence** | Positive/negative skew |
| 🌐 **Network Density** | Conceptual richness |

### 📈 Sample Output

```
Test: Free Association (50 words)
─────────────────────────────────
Avg Reaction Time: 2.1 s
Unique Responses: 47/50
Common Responses: 34/50
Rare Responses:   13/50
Blocks:            2 (Words: "loss", "fear")
─────────────────────────────────
Dominant Themes:
  🟢 Positive: 62%
  🟡 Neutral:  24%
  🔴 Negative: 14%
```

### 🧠 Patterns to Notice

| Pattern | Possible Interpretation |
|---------|------------------------|
| 🔁 Many repeats | Stress or fixation |
| 🚫 Frequent blocks | Emotional interference |
| 🎨 Very rare responses | Creative or divergent thinking |
| 📋 Very common responses | Social conformity |
| ⚡ Fast uniform times | Fluency |
| 🐌 Slow on specific words | Emotional salience |

## 💬 FAQ

**Is this a real psychological test?**
It's based on real methodology but meant for self-exploration, not diagnosis.

**Is my data private?**
Yes — anonymous mode is available.

**How long does it take?**
5–15 minutes depending on the variant.

**Can I retake it?**
Yes — results may vary with mood.

**Are results scientifically valid?**
The method is established, but online self-tests are not clinically standardized.

**Can I use it for research?**
Yes — with proper disclosure and consent.

**Is it free?**
Yes — the platform is free to use.

**Does it work on mobile?**
Yes — responsive design.

**Can I use it with kids?**

**Does it diagnose mental illness?**
No — it's a tool for insight, not diagnosis.
